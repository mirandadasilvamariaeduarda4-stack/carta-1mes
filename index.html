My Love, Carol ❤️

Today I stop to write something that my heart has been screaming since the day you walked into my life: I love you more than anything. More than any word I could write, more than any gesture I could have made. I love you in a way that overflows, that doesn't fit inside me, that makes me want to be better every single day.

This first official month of dating was small in time, but giant in what we built. In so little time, I could already feel how much we've grown together. We learned to truly listen to each other — not just hear, but understand what the other feels, what the other needs. We learned from one another, we shaped each other, we discovered each other. We made mistakes, yes. But we also got things right. And above all of that, we kept loving each other intensely, without fear, without brakes, without hesitation.

During this first month, I could see clearly how much I love you. How much I want you. How much you make my heart pound and leap with joy just by thinking of you. And it was in this month, more than in any other moment, that I could declare with all the certainty in the world: you are, in fact, the woman with whom I want to build a life. Marry. Have children. Build a home. A home that has already begun to exist in your lap.

I feel like the luckiest woman in the world to have you. And I don't say that just to say it. You left Rio de Janeiro to come meet me. You crossed paths, distances, to ask me to be your girlfriend. That is priceless. That cannot be measured. I will never forget what you did for me. To me, that was the greatest act of love I have ever received in my life. And I will carry that with me forever, with gratitude and with a love that only grows.

Carol, you are the best part of me. The best part of my days. Without a doubt, you are the person I love most in the world. And you are not just my girlfriend — you are also my best friend. The person with whom I want to share everything: the victories, the fears, the silences, the laughter, the plans. Everything.

I have come to yearn for and depend entirely on your kiss. The most delicious taste in the world. Your embrace is my best refuge. Your lap is my home. Your touch is everything I desire. Since I saw you in person, I can only repeat in my head how incredibly beautiful you are. Owner of the most beautiful eyes I have ever seen and the most beautiful heart I have ever known. You have the most delicious laugh and a giggle that echoes inside my head every day, saving me whenever I am in the dark.

You are the love of my life. And your fire-red hair remains engraved in my memory as the most beautiful image I have ever seen. When I saw you for the first time, my heart was in ecstasy, trying to understand how I could be so lucky. And how could I deserve her? Because I don't deserve you, you are so good and perfect. And still, I feel like the luckiest human being in the world to have you with me.

I want to be my best version with you. I want that, during all the months to come, we can build a beautiful relationship. That we can continue creating our little world and our story. This was only the first of many months yet to come. And until then, I will be here, loving you and growing with you.

I will be with you not just to be your girlfriend, but to be a support. Someone who will take you by the hand and lift you up. Someone who will teach and learn. Someone who will fix flaws and misunderstandings with love, respect, and a lot of peace. You don't need to be afraid of making mistakes, because afterward we will get it right together. Always together.

I love you so much, Carol. More than any letter can say. More than any month can measure. And if this is only the beginning, I can hardly wait for everything we are still going to live.

With all my love,

Yours. 🏁❤️

The Road We Drive Together 🚗❤️

Verse I — The First Mile

I never knew a heart could start so fast,
Like an engine roaring on a mountain pass.
Then you appeared, and everything stood still,
A fire-red spark that bent my iron will.
They say the road is long, they say it's rough,
But one month with you has been more than enough
To know that I was built for something true —
A life, a home, a highway made for two.

Verse II — The Road We Built Together

We learned to listen, not just hear the sound,
To slow the wheels before we hit the ground.
We made our mistakes, we took a wrong turn or two,
But we always found the road back — me and you.
We grew so much in such a little while,
Learning to brake, to swerve, to reconcile.
We erred, we fixed, we stumbled, then we flew,
And through it all, my love for you stayed true.
I saw it clearly in that first month's drive:
How much I love you, how much I feel alive.
My heart goes vroom and skips and leaps with glee,
Because you are the only road for me.

Verse III — The Woman I Choose

It was in this month I knew with certainty —
You are the woman I was made to see.
The one I'll marry, the one I'll build beside,
A home, a family, a joyful ride.
I am the luckiest woman in the world,
My checkered flag, my victory unfurled.
You drove from Rio just to find me here,
To ask me to be yours — and now it's clear:
No price, no trophy, no grand prix could hold
The weight of what you did, so brave and bold.
I'll never forget it, not for all my days —
The greatest act of love, in all its ways.

Verse IV — The Best Part of Me

Carol, you're the best part of my soul,
The part that makes me whole, that makes me whole.
The best part of my days, my every mile,
The reason for my laughter and my smile.
You're not just my girlfriend — oh, so much more,
My best friend, the one I'm driving for.
I crave your kiss, the sweetest taste I know,
Your arms, the safest place I'll ever go.
Your lap's my home, your touch is my desire,
Your presence sets my weary heart on fire.
Since I saw you, I just can't help but say:
How stunning you are, in every single way.

Verse V — Those Eyes, That Laugh

The most beautiful eyes I've ever seen,
The kindest heart, the softest in-between.
Your laugh, the tastiest sound that fills the air,
Your giggle echoes — I find it everywhere.
It saves me daily when I'm in the dark,
A guiding light, a bright and hopeful spark.
The love of my life — that's what you are,
My fire-red comet, my favorite star.
Your fire-red hair is burned into my mind,
The most beautiful picture I could ever find.
When I first saw you, my heart was in a daze,
Wondering how I earned such grace-filled days.
How could I deserve someone so good, so true?
I don't — and yet the universe gave me you.
I'm the luckiest human being alive,
With you beside me, I can only thrive.

Verse VI — The Promise of the Road Ahead

I want to be my best version with you,
To build a love so beautiful and new.
To keep on crafting our own little world,
A story for the ages, unfurled.
This was just the first of many months to come,
And till the end, I'll be here, never numb.
Loving you, growing with you, side by side,
With you as my companion and my guide.
I'll be with you, not just as your girlfriend,
But as a support until the very end.
Someone who takes your hand and lifts you high,
Who teaches, learns, and never says goodbye.
We'll fix our flaws, our clashes, and our fears,
With love, respect, and peace through all the years.
You need not fear mistakes — we'll make them right,
Together, always, in the warmest light.

Final Verse — The Checkered Flag

I love you more than any words can say,
More than any month could ever weigh.
If this is just the start of our grand race,
I can't wait to see what lies in space.
So here's my heart, my engine, and my all,
I'm yours completely — catch me when I fall.
With every mile and every turn we take,
I'll love you more, for love's own sake.
I love you so much, Carol.

— Yours, on every road, forever.
